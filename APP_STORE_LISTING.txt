🏸 Welcome to IISc Badminton Club's Official App!

Transform your badminton experience with real-time match tracking, dynamic ELO ratings, and community features built for serious players and casual enthusiasts alike.

✨ CORE FEATURES

📊 Live Leaderboards
Track rankings across all competition categories: Men's Singles (MS), Women's Singles (WS), Men's Doubles (MD), Women's Doubles (WD), and Mixed Doubles (XD). Real-time ELO ratings from Bronze to Grandmaster tiers.

🎮 Match Logging
Log your results instantly:
• Singles & Doubles matches
• Friendly & Tournament modes
• Hybrid matches (1v2 format)
• Cross-gender matches
• Mixed-category matches
• Offline mode for gym training

📱 Player Profiles
Comprehensive player statistics:
• Win/loss records by category
• Performance trends & analytics
• Achievement badges & milestones
• Head-to-head matchups
• Detailed match history
• Favorite opponents & buddies

🏆 Advanced Features

• Tournament Management - Organize and track tournament brackets
• Weekly Challenges - Time-limited competitive events with rewards
• Match Predictions - AI-powered win probability estimates
• Umpire Mode - Dedicated interface for match officials
• Dispute Resolution - Admin review of contested matches
• Activity Feed - Real-time notifications and updates
• Announcements - Important club news and events
• Find & Lost - Community board for lost/found items
• Photo Gallery - Tournament photos and highlights
• CSV Export - Download leaderboard data for analysis

🌙 Dark Mode - Comfortable viewing in any lighting
📱 Responsive Design - Perfect on phones, tablets, desktops
🔔 Push Notifications - Match updates, challenges, confirmations
