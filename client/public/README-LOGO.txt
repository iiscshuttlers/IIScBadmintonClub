# Add Your IISc Logo Here

Place your logo file in this folder:
- Filename: iisc-logo.png (or update Navigation.tsx)
- Format: PNG, SVG, or JPG
- Recommended size: 200x200 pixels or larger
- Square or nearly square works best

Your logo will appear in the top-left navigation bar.

See LOGO-CHANGE-GUIDE.md for detailed instructions.
