IISc Badminton Open

Official Tournament Rulebook

1 General Guidelines

  • Eligibility: The tournament is strictly open to the IISc community, including students (UG, PG, PhD, Postdocs), project staff, faculty, and administrative staff. Alumni are not permitted.
  • ID Verification: All participants must carry their valid IISc ID cards during the tournament. The organizing committee reserves the right to verify IDs before any match, at the organizer's discretion.
  • Mandatory Profile & Registration: Creating a player profile and registering for the tournament on the official website/app is strictly mandatory to participate.
  • Official Communication: All tournament updates, match schedules, and official notices will be communicated exclusively through the website/app and the designated WhatsApp groups.
  • Format: The tournament will follow a standard knockout/single-elimination format across all categories (Men's Singles, Women's Singles, Men's Doubles, Women's Doubles, Mixed Doubles) unless stated otherwise.
  • Draws & Seeding: Seeding will be determined by the organizing committee based on past tournament performances and known player rankings. The tournament draw generated by the committee is final and binding. No requests for redraws or opponent changes will be entertained.

2 Match Rules & Scoring

  • BWF Standards: All matches will be played according to the standard rules of the Badminton World Federation (BWF).
  • Scoring System: Preliminary rounds will be played as a single set of 30 points (change of ends at 15 points) to ensure the tournament stays on schedule. Quarter-finals, Semi-finals, and Finals will be played as best of three (3) sets of 21 points or 15 points.
    *Note: The organizing committee reserves the right to alter the scoring format based on time constraints, strictly at the organizer's discretion.
  • Shuttlecocks: Official matches will be played strictly with Plastic Shuttlecocks (Yonex Mavis 350) provided by the organizers. Requests to use feather shuttles or personal shuttles will not be entertained.

3 Scheduling & Walkovers

  • Reporting Time: Players must report to the registration desk at least 15 minutes before their scheduled match time.
  • Strict No-Delay Policy: If a player or team fails to report to their designated court within 10 minutes of the match being announced, a Walkover (W/O) will be awarded to the opponent, at the organizer's discretion.
  • Back-to-Back Matches: Players participating in multiple categories may face back-to-back matches. While organizers will try to provide adequate rest, players must comply with the schedule without raising concerns or requesting delays.
  • Schedule Changes: No requests for schedule modifications will be entertained once the schedule is released for that day. Even if a request is made prior to the schedule release, the organizing committee may not be able to accommodate it due to strict tournament constraints.

4 Equipment & Discipline

  • Footwear: Non-marking badminton shoes are strictly mandatory. Players wearing running shoes, casual shoes, or marking soles will not be allowed on the courts and will face immediate disqualification, at the organizer's discretion.
  • Apparel: Standard sports attire (t-shirts, shorts/skirts) must be worn. Players must bring their own rackets.
  • Umpire's Decision: Matches will be officiated by designated umpires. The decision of the umpire is final and binding.
  • Conduct: Any form of misconduct, use of abusive language, aggressive behavior towards opponents or umpires, or racket abuse will result in immediate disqualification.

5 Medical & Injury

  • Fitness: By registering, players declare they are medically fit to participate.
  • Injuries: In the event of an on-court injury, a maximum of a 5-minute medical timeout is permitted, at the umpire's discretion. If the player cannot continue after this time, the match will be forfeited. The organizing committee is not liable for any injuries sustained during the tournament.
By registering for the tournament, participants agree to abide by all the rules and regulations mentioned above. The organizing committee reserves the right to amend the rules if necessary for the smooth functioning of the event, strictly at the organizer's discretion.
