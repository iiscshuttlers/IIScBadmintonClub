## Getting Started

### Installation

1. **Download the app** from the Google Play Store (search "IISc Shuttlers")
2. **Open the app** and tap "Sign Up"
3. **Enter your email** and create a secure password (min 8 characters)
4. **Choose a nickname** (your player name, min 3 characters)
5. **Verify your email** by clicking the link sent to your inbox
6. **Complete your profile** with your details

### First Login

- On first login, you'll be guided through the onboarding tour
- You'll see the home screen with tabs for Feed, Leaderboard, Players, and more
- Your profile is automatically created with a starting ELO of 1200

## Creating Your Profile

### Edit Your Details

1. **Tap your profile icon** (bottom right of most screens)
2. **Tap "Edit Profile"**
3. **Update your information**:
   - **Nickname**: Your display name
   - **Gender**: M, F, or Other (affects matching)
   - **Level**: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Professional
   - **Hand**: R (Right) or L (Left)
   - **Avatar**: Upload or take a photo
   - **Playing Style**: Your preferred shots (optional)
   - **Racket Details**: Racket model (optional)

4. **Tap "Save"** to save changes

### Profile Visibility

- Your **public profile** includes:
  - Name, avatar, gender, level
  - Match history
  - ELO ratings
  - Win-loss record
  - Achievements

- **Private information** (not public):
  - Email address
  - Password
  - Device information

## Logging Matches

### Quick Log Match

1. **From any screen**, tap the **"+"** floating button (usually bottom right)
2. **Tap "Log Match"**
3. **Select your opponent** (search by name)
4. **Choose the match format**:
   - **MS**: Men's Singles
   - **WS**: Women's Singles
   - **MD**: Men's Doubles
   - **WD**: Women's Doubles
   - **XD**: Mixed Doubles

5. **Enter the score**:
   - First number: Your score
   - Second number: Opponent's score
   - Valid scores: 0-30 for each set
   - Minimum: 21 points to win
   - Win with 2-point margin

6. **Select match type**:
   - **Competitive**: Affects your ELO rating
   - **Friendly**: For fun, no ELO change

7. **Add details** (optional):
   - Match duration
   - Court location
   - Video link
   - Notes

8. **Tap "Log Match"** to save

### Multiple Sets

For best-of-3 matches:
1. Enter the score for set 1
2. Tap "Add Set"
3. Enter set 2 score
4. If needed, add set 3
5. The app automatically determines the winner

## Understanding ELO Ratings

### What is ELO?

ELO is a rating system that measures your badminton skill level:
- **Starting rating**: 1200
- **Updates after every competitive match**
- **Higher rating = Better rank**

### How ELO Changes

#### When You Win:
- **Expected win** (against lower-rated player): +10-15 points
- **Upset win** (against higher-rated player): +20-32 points

#### When You Lose:
- **Expected loss** (against higher-rated player): -10-15 points
- **Surprising loss** (against lower-rated player): -20-32 points

#### Special Cases:
- **Friendly matches**: No ELO change
- **Tournament matches**: May have multipliers
- **Best-of-3**: 1.5x multiplier applied

### ELO Categories

The app tracks separate ELO ratings for:
1. **MS** (Men's Singles)
2. **WS** (Women's Singles)
3. **MD** (Men's Doubles)
4. **WD** (Women's Doubles)
5. **XD** (Mixed Doubles)
6. **Blended**: Average of all categories (for overall ranking)

### ELO Tiers

| Tier | ELO Range | Color |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Bronze | < 1000 | Amber |
| Silver | 1000-1299 | Gray |
| Gold | 1300-1599 | Yellow |
| Platinum | 1600-1899 | Cyan |
| Diamond | 1900-2199 | Indigo |
| Grandmaster | 2200+ | Rose |

## Using the Leaderboard

### View Leaderboard

1. **Tap the "Leaderboard" tab** (usually 2nd or 3rd tab)
2. **Filter by format**:
   - Blended (overall ranking)
   - MS, WS, MD, WD, XD (format-specific)
   - Or combine formats

3. **Filter by period**:
   - All-time
   - This season
   - This month
   - This week

4. **Find your rank**: Scroll or search for your name

### Leaderboard Information

Each player shows:
- **Rank**: Position in leaderboard (1st, 2nd, etc.)
- **Name**: Player nickname
- **ELO**: Current rating
- **Wins/Losses**: Match record
- **Win Rate**: Percentage of matches won

### Tie-Breaking

When two players have the same ELO:
1. Higher win rate wins
2. If tie-break still needed, head-to-head record
3. Most recent match result

   - By name
   - By gender (M, F, Other)
   - By level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced)

   - Win-loss record

1. **Tap on any player card**
2. **See tabs**:
   - **Stats**: Win rate, records by category
   - **Matches**: Match history against all opponents
   - **Progression**: ELO trend chart
   - **Head-to-Head**: Your record against this player
   - **Achievements**: Unlocked badges and milestones

- **Match notifications** when it's your turn

- Must have completed profile setup
- Must accept the tournament terms
- Cannot drop out after round starts (penalties apply)

## Offline Mode

### Using Offline

**The app works offline!** You can:
- View cached match history
- See leaderboard snapshots
- View profiles you've visited
- Queue matches to log later

**You cannot**:
- Access real-time data
- See live leaderboard updates
- View current tournaments
- Send messages

### Logging Matches Offline

1. **Tap "+" → Log Match"** (same as usual)
2. **Enter match details**
3. **Tap "Log Match"**
4. **Match is queued** (you'll see "pending sync" badge)

### Syncing When Online

When you regain internet:
1. **App automatically syncs** your queued matches
2. **See sync status** in status bar
3. **If sync fails**: Try manually syncing from settings
4. **All data is preserved** during offline period

## FAQs & Troubleshooting

### I can't log in. What should I do?

1. **Check internet connection**
2. **Verify email**: Make sure you verified your email
3. **Forgot password**: Tap "Forgot Password" on login
4. **Clear cache**: Go to Settings → App Storage → Clear Cache
5. **Contact support**: Email support@iiscshuttlers.github.io

### Why didn't my ELO change after a match?

**Possible reasons**:
- **Friendly match**: Friendly matches don't affect ELO
- **Not yet processed**: It can take a few seconds to update
- **Match in tournament**: Tournament ELO updates when tournament ends
- **Data syncing**: Wait for sync to complete

### How do I recover a deleted match?

1. **Contact admin** with match details
2. **Provide**: Date, opponent, score
3. **Admins can restore** within 30 days
4. **After 30 days**: Permanently deleted

### My profile picture won't upload. What's wrong?

1. **Check file size**: Image should be < 5MB
2. **Check format**: JPG, PNG, or WebP supported
3. **Check permissions**: Grant camera and photo permissions
4. **Try again**: Wait a moment and try uploading again
5. **Contact support**: If still failing

### Can I play with someone not on the app?

**Not directly**, but:
1. **Invite them to join**: Share your profile link
2. **They create an account**
3. **Then log match together**

### How do I report a player for misconduct?

1. **View their profile**
2. **Tap "..." menu** (if available)
3. **Tap "Report"**
4. **Describe the issue**
5. **Admins review** and take action

### The leaderboard isn't updating. Why?

1. **Refresh**: Pull down to refresh
2. **Check internet**: Ensure connected
3. **Wait**: Updates take 5-10 minutes
4. **Restart app**: Close and reopen
5. **Contact support**: If still not updating

### Can I change my nickname?

**Yes**, limited times:
1. **Go to Edit Profile**
2. **Change nickname**
3. **Save**
4. **Note**: You can only change it 2 times per month

### What happens if I delete my account?

- **30-day grace period**: You can restore your account
- **After 30 days**: Permanently deleted
- **Match history**: Retained anonymously for statistics
- **Data export**: Download your data before deleting

### Is my data private?

**Yes**:
- Your email is never shared
- Your password is encrypted
- Match data is secure
- Only admin can see your private info
- You control who sees your public profile
